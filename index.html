How to use the AI text humanizer

  1. 1Paste your text. Copy AI-written text into the left box, drop a .txt file on it, or tap Upload. Try the sample to see it work.
  2. 2Pick a tool. Humanizer for natural tone, Paraphraser to reword, Grammar Fixer to clean mistakes, Summarizer to shorten.
  3. 3Choose style and strength. Light keeps most of your wording. Strong rewrites heavily. Medium suits most text.
  4. 4Press the button. The first time, allow the small Puter window so the AI can run.
  5. 5Review, edit, copy or download. The result box is editable. Fix anything that changed your meaning, then use Copy or Download.

Tips for better results

  • Paste a full paragraph or more. Very short text has little to improve.
  • Add your own details, examples or opinions after humanizing. Real specifics are what make writing sound human.
  • Run Light first on text you like, and Strong on stiff or robotic text.
  • Not happy? Press the button again for a fresh version, or use "Use as input" to refine the result further.
  • Always proofread names, numbers and quotes.

Frequently asked questions

Is this AI humanizer really free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no word allowance. The site is supported by ads.

Will it bypass AI detectors?

No tool can guarantee that. It makes text read more naturally, but you should always review and edit the result yourself. Many schools and sites ban undisclosed AI writing.

How long does it take?

Usually a few seconds. Long texts are processed in parts, so a 3,000 word document can take up to a minute.

Do you store my text?

No. Your text is sent to an AI model to be rewritten. Your recent history is saved only in your own browser.

What languages are supported?

English works best. Spanish, Portuguese, French and German also work. The result is written in the same language as your input.
